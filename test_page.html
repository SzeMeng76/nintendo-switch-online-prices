Family Group Settings

Cancelling Automatic Renewal

If you or a member of your family group purchases or redeems a new membership while automatic renewal is active for one or more existing memberships, automatic renewal for any unnecessary memberships will be cancelled.

If the membership you purchase or redeem is an individual membership (with Expansion Pack)

Automatic renewal will be cancelled for:

・Individual membership (without Expansion Pack)

If the membership you purchase or redeem is a family membership (without Expansion Pack)

Automatic renewal will be cancelled for:

・Individual membership (without Expansion Pack)
・Family group members' individual memberships (without Expansion Pack)
・Family group members' individual memberships (with Expansion Pack)

If the membership you purchase or redeem is a family membership (with Expansion Pack)

Automatic renewal will be cancelled for:

・Individual membership (without Expansion Pack)
・Family group members' individual memberships (without Expansion Pack)
・Individual membership (with Expansion Pack)
・Family group members' individual memberships (with Expansion Pack)
・Family membership (without Expansion Pack)
・Family group members' family memberships (without Expansion Pack)

If multiple users with automatic renewal active for Nintendo Switch Online memberships join the same family group, automatic renewal will be cancelled as displayed above.
If multiple members of a family group have automatic renewal active for the same type of family membership, automatic renewal will be cancelled for the user whose membership expires soonest.
Automatic renewal will be cancelled on the expiry date of the affected membership.

Once you have purchased or redeemed a membership, you can start using it immediately. The validity period of your membership will be extended if you purchase or redeem a new membership before it expires.

Automatic renewal for this product is time-based.
